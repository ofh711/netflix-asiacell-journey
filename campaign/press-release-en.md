# For Immediate Release

## Asiacell and Netflix Launch Direct-Carrier Billing in Iraq

**Iraqi customers can now subscribe to Netflix and pay directly from their Asiacell mobile balance — a first for the Iraqi market.**

**Baghdad, Iraq — May 10, 2026** — Asiacell, Iraq's largest mobile telecommunications operator, today announced a strategic partnership with Netflix that enables customers to subscribe to the world's leading streaming service and have the cost charged directly to their Asiacell mobile balance. The launch makes Iraq one of the first markets in the region to offer Netflix subscriptions through direct-carrier billing.

Effective immediately, both prepaid and postpaid Asiacell customers can subscribe to any Netflix plan in three steps from their phone: visit **netflix.com/iq**, choose a plan, and select Asiacell as the payment method. The subscription cost is then deducted from their mobile balance, with no credit card required.

> "Our customers told us clearly: they want premium entertainment, and they want it without the friction of credit cards or international payment systems," said the CEO of Asiacell. "This partnership with Netflix delivers exactly that. With more than 17 million Iraqis on the Asiacell network, we're making it dramatically easier to access world-class shows and films — paid directly from a balance our customers already trust."

The collaboration reflects Netflix's expanding investment in MENA storytelling and its continued push to make the service accessible across diverse payment ecosystems.

> "Iraq is home to a vibrant audience that's hungry for the kinds of stories Netflix tells — from local Arabic productions to global hits," said the Director of Business Development at Netflix MENA. "By partnering with Asiacell, the country's most trusted mobile brand, we're removing one of the biggest barriers to streaming and bringing Netflix closer to millions of new viewers."

### How it works

1. Visit **netflix.com/iq** from any browser.
2. Sign in with email and select a subscription plan.
3. Choose **Asiacell** as the payment method, enter the mobile number, and confirm with the OTP code sent via SMS.

The amount is deducted from the Asiacell balance, and the subscription is activated instantly. A confirmation SMS is sent to the customer's phone for every transaction.

### About Asiacell

Asiacell is Iraq's leading mobile telecommunications operator with more than 17 million subscribers across all 18 governorates. Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Sulaimaniyah, the company offers nationwide voice, data, and digital services and has been a long-standing pioneer of mobile connectivity in Iraq. Asiacell has held the title of "Iraq's Most Trusted Telecom Brand" for several consecutive years.

### About Netflix

Netflix is one of the world's leading entertainment services with subscribers in more than 190 countries enjoying TV series, films and games across a wide variety of genres and languages. Members can play, pause and resume watching as much as they want, anytime, anywhere, and can change their plans at any time.
